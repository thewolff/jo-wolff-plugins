**Precedence.** A row here beats every rule in `rules.md` and every note in `frameworks.md`. When a rule would fire on
something listed here, the skill does not report it as a finding or an observation; it writes a do-not-flag note under
"Do-not-flag notes that fired" in the report, with the row id and the source. Example: FW-NEXT-09 and FW-NEXT-10 locate
`app/robots.ts` and `app/sitemap.ts`, but their absence is not reported (DNF-16, DNF-11).

Every row cites a primary source fetched in this workload; its `quote` appears verbatim in a file under `research/`, and
its `source URL` is listed in `sources.md`. `planted-in` names the control fixture path that carries the myth when it is
mechanically checkable; `test/coverage.test.ts` requires that control's expected occurrences (`expect/<family>.json`) to carry the row's dnf-note and no
finding at that locator.

Aliases: performance research `DNF-NN` ships as `PERF-DNF-NN`, frameworks research `DNF-NN` as `FW-DNF-NN`;
google-core `DNF-NN`, structured-data `NF-NN` and ai-crawlers-bing `NF-AI-N`/`NF-BNG-N` keep their research ids.
